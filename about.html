About

SEO for people who actually write

Bloggists exists because good writers keep getting told their traffic problem is a content problem. Usually it isn't.

We work with bloggers and small businesses whose sites are technically invisible: never submitted to Search Console, sitemaps that point nowhere, duplicate titles, orphan posts with no internal links, themes that block crawlers by accident.

Fixing that is unglamorous work. It's also the difference between publishing into a void and building traffic that compounds. That's the boulder — the push is real, but once the foundation is set, momentum does the rest.

Paid ads get more crowded and expensive every month. Renting traffic from platforms means paying forever and hoping the auction doesn't price you out. Configuring your SEO builds owned visibility — the work compounds, the clicks don't stop when the budget does, and the people who find you are actively looking for exactly what you wrote.

We specialize in Blogger.com, where most agencies won't go, and work comfortably in WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, and Shopify. Alongside client work we run a network of state-specific sites used for local SEO placements.

Solid foundation. Sustainable growth.
